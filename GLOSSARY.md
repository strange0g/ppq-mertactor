# Past Paper Compiler (ppq-mertactor)

Extracts Cambridge past paper documents from Google Drive and compiles them into structured single-year revision booklets and examiner reports.

## Language

**Component**:
An examination paper number within a syllabus (e.g., Paper 1, Paper 2, Paper 3, Paper 4).
_Avoid_: Paper type, exam unit

**Series**:
An examination session held in a specific period of the year (e.g., February/March, May/June, October/November).
_Avoid_: Season, exam period, sitting

**Variant**:
A regional edition of a question paper or mark scheme within a series, denoted by a two-digit code where the second digit is the region (e.g., 11, 12, 13).
_Avoid_: Version, zone paper

**Question Paper (QP)**:
The examination question document presented to candidates.
_Avoid_: Exam sheet, test paper

**Mark Scheme (MS)**:
The official grading criteria and model answers published by the exam board for a specific question paper variant.
_Avoid_: Answer key, solution sheet

**Examiner Report (ER)**:
A consolidated report published after an exam series detailing candidate performance, common misconceptions, and guidance.
_Avoid_: Chief examiner comments, performance review

**Insert (IN)**:
A supplementary booklet containing case studies, data, or texts required to answer questions in specific components.
_Avoid_: Case study booklet, appendix

**Component Compilation**:
A single collated PDF for a specific component and year, interleaving each variant's insert, question paper, and matching mark scheme across all series.
_Avoid_: Master booklet, year bundle

**Examiner Reports Compilation**:
A consolidated single PDF merging all series examiner reports for a given syllabus and year.
_Avoid_: Combined feedback, year report
